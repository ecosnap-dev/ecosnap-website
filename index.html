Our Objective

Plastic waste is one of the world's most pressing health and environmental concerns. Despite high awareness, nearly two-thirds of Malaysia's waste is still disposed of improperly, leading to a national recycling rate of only 33% and significant microplastic contamination. EcoSnap+ aims to bridge this gap by replacing outdated, voluntary systems with AI-driven smart bins that eliminate sorting errors and foul odours. By integrating digital tracking and EcoPoint rewards, we transform recycling from an inconsistent chore into a measurable, incentivised habit.
